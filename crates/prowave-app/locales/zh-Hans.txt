Space plays and stops. Drag a file onto the window to add it. = 按空格键播放和停止。将文件拖到窗口即可添加。
To record audio, pick the input in the track's Input slot, press R, then Record and Play. = 录制音频时，请在轨道的 Input 栏选择输入，按 R，然后按录音和播放。
To record MIDI, press R on an instrument track, then Record and Play. = 录制 MIDI 时，请在乐器轨道上按 R，然后按录音和播放。
The I button lets you hear an audio track's input. Use headphones to avoid feedback. = 按 I 按钮可以听到音频轨道的输入。请使用耳机以避免啸叫。
With Count-in on, recording starts after one bar of clicks. = 打开预备拍后，录音会在一小节的节拍声后开始。
Capture Recording turns what you just played on a MIDI keyboard into a region. = 捕捉录音会把你刚在 MIDI 键盘上弹奏的内容变成一个片段。
