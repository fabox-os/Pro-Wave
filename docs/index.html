Make music.Not excuses.

Pro Wave is a Logic Pro–style DAW written in pure Rust. Tracks, MIDI, audio, a Mixer with real effects and automation — drawn with egui and played through cpal. No Electron. No JUCE.

What you can do today

An early but working studio. The full plan is in the roadmap; this is what already runs.

Arrange

Tracks & regions

MIDI, audio, Drummer and Pattern regions; cut, copy, paste, split, join, bounce and Bounce in Place.

Record

MIDI & audio

Record from your keyboard or interface, with count-in, cycle, MIDI clock and time code sync.

Edit

Piano Roll & Score

Quantize, velocity lane, Event List, Step Sequencer and a Score view with MusicXML export.

Mix

Mixer & automation

Channel strips, sends, buses, VCA, groups, delay compensation and automation for faders, effects and sends.

Effects

Real DSP

Channel EQ, Compressor, Multi-Band Compressor, Space Designer convolution reverb, Amp Designer, delays, modulation and more — on any strip, even the Stereo Out.

Sound

Instruments

Built-in synth, SoundFont (.sf2) instruments and Hydrogen drum kits, plus the Library of patches.

Flex

Flex & Smart Tempo

Flex Time and Pitch on audio, tempo detection and warping.

Import

Your projects

MIDI files (SMPTE and markers too), audio files, and GarageBand project audio.

See it

The look and workflow follow Logic Pro: dark panels, Library, Inspector, Tracks area and Mixer.

Piano Roll with quantize and velocity lane

Playback with live channel meters

Light and real-time

The audio thread never locks, allocates or touches the disk. The UI talks to the engine only through a lock-free command queue.

~15 MB program

Starts in a few hundred milliseconds and idles around 120 MB of memory.

Sample-accurate

Transport, tempo map and automation run on the audio clock, with plug-in delay compensation.

64-bit summing

Tracks are summed in double precision before the master fader.

Honest status

Pro Wave is alpha software. Plug-in hosting, Live Loops and much of Logic's instrument list are still to come. Every release lists what was tested and what was not.
